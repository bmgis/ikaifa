# Privacy Policy — IKAIFA: Learn to Read

**Effective Date:** August 4, 2026

## Overview

IKAIFA respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app operates entirely on your device.

---

## Information We Collect

**IKAIFA does not collect any personal data.**

The app does **not**:

- Require user registration or account creation
- Collect your name, email address, or other personal information
- Use cookies, tracking technologies, or analytics services
- Display third-party advertisements
- Send data to external servers
- Access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or other sensitive permissions

All learning progress is stored locally on your Android device using a Room database. This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time by using the **"Reset Progress"** feature within the app or by uninstalling the app.

---

## Audio

IKAIFA uses pre-recorded audio files included with the application to support learning. The app does not record your voice or transmit any audio data.

---

## In-App Purchases

IKAIFA offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium content.

All payments are processed securely through **Google Play Billing** and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. IKAIFA does not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, including credit card or bank account details.

---

## Children's Privacy

IKAIFA is designed to be used by children under the guidance of a parent, guardian, or teacher.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from users of any age. If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the information provided below.

## Third-Party Links

IKAIFA does not contain links to external websites or third-party services within the app.
